Our solution

Decode → Rehearse → Reflect → Retry

1
DecodeWhat phrases may mean, the power dynamics, what you can't know, questions to ask.
2
RehearseA character with private motives who pushes back in proportion to how you handle it.
3
ReflectFeedback built on your own quoted words. Interpretations framed as possibilities.
4
RetryOne specific challenge, then a side-by-side comparison of both attempts.

Honestly: AI role-play already exists (Yoodli, Second Nature), and Huru's feedback already quotes users. Our focus is decoding unwritten expectations and feedback the server verifies.

Agent architecture

Three agents, one orchestrator, read-only tools

Each tool takes one enumerated argument and reads curated data already in memory. No tool can open a file. Instructions, scenario data and user text travel in separate channels.

The future of SkillSim

From prototype to a library of unwritten rules

Limits today

  • Local prototype, text only, 3 scenarios
  • Feedback checked for form, not yet for truth
  • Knowledge base: US-centric, one author
  • Character can agree to a figure the user names

Next month

  • Usability study with ≥5 people from the target groups
  • Career advisor rates the outputs blind
  • Scenario authoring with career centers
  • Deploy with accounts and encrypted storage; then voice

The goal: the conversations nobody teaches, decoded honestly and practiced until they feel familiar.
